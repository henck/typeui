# Label: Variations

.

<Meta of={LabelVariationsStories} />

## basic

A `basic` Label has a border and a white background. Hovering labels with `onClick` only affects labels with default color.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Basic} />
</Canvas>

## color

A label can have a background `color`. If the `color` property is set, then
the font will turn white and bold.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Color} />
</Canvas>

## positive

A label can have a `positive` color. If the `positive` property is set, then
the font will turn white and bold.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Positive} />
</Canvas>

## negative

A label can have a `negative` color. If the `negative` property is set, then
the font will turn white and bold.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Negative} />
</Canvas>

## floating

A label can appear `floating` above another component.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Floating} />
</Canvas>

## pointing

A label can be `pointing` to `top`, `bottom`, `left` or `right`.

  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Pointing} />

## size

A `Label` can have a `size`.

  <Story of={LabelVariationsStories.Size} />

A `Label` can appear as a `tag`. Tagged labels are meant to be placed on the right side of anything they're attached to.
