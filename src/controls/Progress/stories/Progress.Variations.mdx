# Progress: Variations

.

<Meta of={ProgressVariationsStories} />

## background

A `Progress` with the `background` attribute adds a background to the progress bar.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Background} />
</Canvas>

## bordered

A `bordered` `Progress` adds a border around the progress bar.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Bordered} />
</Canvas>

## color

By default, a `Progress` has the primary theme color. You can override this using
the `color` attribute.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Color} />
</Canvas>

## numbered

A `numbered` `Progress` shows a percentage value.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Numbered} />
</Canvas>

## raised

A `raised` `Progress` adds a drop shadow. This works well with the `background` attribute.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Raised} />
</Canvas>

## rectangular

A `rectangular` `Progress` has sharp corners rather than rounded ones.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Rectangular} />
</Canvas>

## thickness

`thickness` can be used to create thicker `Progress` bars.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Thickness} />
</Canvas>

## padded

A `padded` `Progress` has a margin around it.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={ProgressVariationsStories.Padded} />
</Canvas>
